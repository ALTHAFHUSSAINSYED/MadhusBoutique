(Project Settings -> Environment Variables):

1. NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL
   - Format: https://[your-project-id].supabase.co
   - Scope: Production, Preview, Development (Public browser)

2. NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY
   - Format: eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9...
   - Scope: Production, Preview, Development (Public browser for reading active products)

3. SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY (CRITICAL SECRET)
   - Format: eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9...
   - Scope: Strictly Server-Side. Never expose to client browser.

4. AWS_REGION
   - e.g. ap-south-1 (Asia Pacific - Mumbai)
   - Scope: Server-Side

5. AWS_S3_BUCKET
   - e.g. madhus-boutique-private (With Block All Public Access set to ON)
   - Scope: Server-Side

6. AWS_ACCESS_KEY_ID
   - Dedicated IAM user credentials with least-privilege S3 access
   - Scope: Server-Side

7. AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY (CRITICAL SECRET)
   - Associated secret for the IAM user. Never exposed to browser.
   - Scope: Server-Side

8. NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL
   - e.g. https://madhusboutique.com (or https://your-project.vercel.app)
   - Scope: Production, Preview, Development

9. ADMIN_SESSION_SECRET (CRITICAL SECRET)
   - Cryptographic random secret used for elevated admin sessions and token signing
   - Scope: Server-Side

SECTION 2: STORAGE DIVISION (S3 vs. POSTGRESQL)
================================================================================

Strict separation of concerns between object storage and relational database:

USE AWS S3 (100% PRIVATE BUCKET) EXCLUSIVELY FOR:
  1. Embroidery machine files (.DST, .PES, .JEF, .EXP):
     Key: products/{product-code}/files/{FORMAT}/{basename}-{nonce}.{ext}
  2. Watermarked design preview images:
     Key: products/{product-code}/preview/{code}-preview-{nonce}.{ext}
  3. Pre-packaged order ZIP archives:
     Key: orders/{order-number}/downloads/{order-number}-designs.zip
  4. Business payment UPI QR graphics:
     Key: payment/qr/upi-qr-{timestamp}-{nonce}.{ext}

USE SUPABASE POSTGRESQL FOR:
  1. Customers (Guest profiles, phone, email, notes)
  2. Product metadata (Name, slug, category, dimensions, stitch count, price)
  3. Orders (Order lifecycle, subtotal, totals, download quotas, expiry)
  4. Order Items (Immutable price snapshots)
  5. Payments (12-digit UTR, payment method, amount, status)
  6. Statuses (order_status, payment_status enums)
  7. Admin profiles (Roles: SUPER_ADMIN, ORDER_MANAGER, CONTENT_MANAGER, MFA state)
  8. Audit logs (Append-only administrative audit trail)
  9. Download logs (Access tracking, IP, timestamp)

SECTION 3: DIGITAL "INVENTORY" & PRODUCT LIFECYCLE
================================================================================

Since Madhus Boutique sells digital embroidery design files, traditional physical 
inventory ("stock = 10") does not apply to digital assets.

Digital Product Statuses:
  - ACTIVE: Listed in public boutique storefront and available for purchase.
  - DRAFT: Work-in-progress design visible only to Content Managers and Super Admins.
  - ARCHIVED: Legacy design retired from public catalogue.
  - OUT_OF_SALE: Temporarily withdrawn from purchase without archiving.

Physical Embroidery Services Extension:
  - If Madhus Boutique introduces physical bespoke blouse stitching or physical 
    garment sales in a future milestone, an 'inventory_quantity' field will be 
    added specifically for physical SKU variants.
  - Physical inventory semantics are never forced onto digital design assets.

SECTION 4: PAYMENT ROADMAP & PRICING TRANSPARENCY
================================================================================

VERSION 1 (CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION):
  - Direct UPI QR presentation + customer UTR submission.
  - Server-side administrative payment verification.
  - Zero payment aggregator transaction percentage fees for initial launch.
  - No gateway onboarding delays.

VERSION 2 (FUTURE MILESTONE):
  - Integration with automated payment providers (Razorpay / Cashfree).
  - Note: Razorpay offers zero setup/AMC and standard transaction-based pricing.
  - Standard UPI has zero MDR, but payment platforms maintain technology/platform 
    fees according to standard pricing structures.
  - Avoid calling payment infrastructure "free payment gateway"—both V1 and V2 
    operate with clear, realistic business economics.
